# Your Privacy Choices & Data Rights — Zurely

**Last Updated:** August 2026  
**Applicable Platform:** Zurely Mobile App (iOS / Android) & Web Portal

At **Zurely**, we respect your right to control your personal data. In compliance with Apple’s App Store Review Guidelines, the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) of Malaysia, and international privacy standards, this page explains how you can manage, correct, export, limit, or delete the data we collect from you.

## 1. Summary of Your Privacy Rights

As a Zurely user (Landlord, Tenant, or Agent), you have the right to:
* **Access & Review** the personal data we store about you.
* **Update & Correct** inaccurate or outdated profile and tenancy details.
* **Delete Your Account & Associated Personal Data** at any time directly in the app.
* **Control Communications** and opt out of promotional messages.
* **Request Data Export** of your tenancy history and payment receipts.

## 2. How to Exercise Your Choices

### A. Modify or Update Your Information
You can update your personal information directly within the Zurely App:
1. Open the **Zurely App**.
2. Navigate to **Profile / Account Settings**.
3. Edit your **Name, Phone Number, Bank account details, Nationality, Current address**.
4. Save the changes.  


### B. In-App Account & Data Deletion (Direct Deletion)
In compliance with Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v), you can delete your account and associated personal data directly within the app without contacting customer support:

1. Log in to your **Zurely App**.
2. Go to **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Security & Privacy** (or **Account Settings**).
3. Scroll to the bottom and select **"Delete Account"**.
4. Confirm your identity via OTP / Password authentication.
5. Confirm the deletion request.

#### What happens when you delete your account:
* Your profile, login credentials, and session tokens will be permanently removed from our active database.
* Marketing and push notification subscriptions will cease immediately.
* Any uncompleted drafts of Tenancy Agreements will be permanently deleted.

### C. Legal & Regulatory Retention Exceptions
Under Malaysian law (including Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia / LHDN stamp duty regulations, the Stamp Act 1949, and accounting/tax compliance standards):
* **Completed & Stamped Tenancy Agreements**, as well as financial transaction records (Stamping fees, rental transactions), must be retained in an encrypted, archived state for the statutory legal retention period (typically up to **7 years**).
* These records will remain locked, inaccessible for marketing or profiling purposes, and accessible only if required by Malaysian tax authorities or court orders.

### D. Managing WhatsApp Notifications & Communications
Zurely uses **WhatsApp messages** to deliver essential transaction alerts, including:
* Tenancy agreement signing reminders and OTP verification
* Stamping status updates (LHDN submission confirmation)
* Payment confirmations and rental receipts

#### Your Choices:
* **Transactional WhatsApp Messages:** These are critical to the legal execution of your agreements and account security. You can stop receiving them at any time by deleting your account.
* **Marketing & Promotional Messages:** If you receive non-essential marketing messages via WhatsApp, you can opt out at any time by replying **`STOP`** in the WhatsApp chat or toggling communication preferences in your Account Settings.
* **No Third-Party Ad Tracking:** We **never** sell your phone number or personal data to third parties or advertising networks.


## 3. Submit a Privacy / Data Request via Email

If you cannot access the app, wish to submit a full Data Subject Access Request (DSAR), or have specific concerns about your data, you can reach out directly to our Data Protection Team:

* **Email:** [privacy@zurely.com](mailto:privacy@zurely.com) *(or your actual support email)*
* **Subject Line:** `Data Privacy Request - [Your Registered Email / Phone]`
* **Response Time:** We will acknowledge your request within **48 hours** and process your request within **14 business days**.
